✦ About

Crafted with Light
and Precision

Shengjie Glass is a contemporary glass art studio based in Shijiazhuang, China. We merge traditional glassblowing techniques with modern design to create objects that are both timeless and avant-garde.

Every piece is hand-finished by master artisans, ensuring that no two are exactly alike. We serve collectors, interior designers, and retailers worldwide with a commitment to quality and authenticity.
